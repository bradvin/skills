## Portable method

- Choose an angle worth opening before investing in a long draft.
- Coordinate banner, title, and intro so they address complementary reader desires or objections.
- Make the reading path clear: visual attention, useful promise, relevant preview, decision to read.
- Show how a process works so readers can judge the reasoning and apply it themselves.
- Build from an existing post, resource, permitted case study, or sanitized procedure.
- Reframe the article in quote-posts with another useful benefit, objection, or genuine new evidence.
- Offer a relevant next action rather than assuming every reader is ready for a sales conversation.

These are editorial heuristics, not evidence that a specific package will produce a particular result.

## Deliberate adaptations

The skill treats process articles as a context-sensitive default and later promotion as evidence-led reframing, not unlimited reposting. It adds existing-draft intake, author-voice preservation, bounded corroboration, disclosure consent, image privacy review, truthful status distinctions, complete revision delivery, measurable learning, and separate publication approval. These safeguards are contributions of this adaptation, not claims about what Chan prescribed.
